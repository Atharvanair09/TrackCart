Market Basket Analysis & Product Combos

Discover product affinities across completed transactions using Support, Confidence, and Lift algorithms.

Production Transactions
🛍️

Association Rule Definitions

Support(A, B) = Transactions with both A & B / Total Transactions  |  Confidence(A → B) = Transactions with A & B / Transactions with A  |  Lift(A, B) = Support(A, B) / (Support(A) × Support(B)). A Lift > 1.0 indicates products are purchased together significantly more often than random chance.
